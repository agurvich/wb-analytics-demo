# Methodology

This document describes the analytical methodology used across WB poverty
and inequality indicators.


## Poverty gap index

The poverty gap index reports the mean shortfall below the poverty line,
expressed as a share of the line and averaged over the poor rather than
over the whole population (the FGT(1) convention). Figures published
before the 2026 Q1 release used the whole-population denominator and are
not comparable.
